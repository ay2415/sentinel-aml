# Liffey Payments - Account Restriction Policy (Restricted)

## Authority
Only the MLRO may restrict an e-money account for financial crime reasons. Restrictions are applied to outbound transfers and card spend, while inbound fraud recalls continue to be processed.

## Review
Restrictions are reviewed within 3 business days and either lifted, extended with documented rationale, or followed by relationship exit.

## Communication
Customers are told only that the account is under review. Staff must not refer to reporting or investigations.
