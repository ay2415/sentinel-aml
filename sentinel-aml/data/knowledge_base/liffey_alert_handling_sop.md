# Liffey Payments - Alert Handling and Approval SOP

## Scope
Liffey Payments is an e-money institution serving consumers and small merchants. Alerts from the transaction monitoring system are handled by the compliance operations team.

## Service levels
Critical alerts within 24 hours, high within 2 business days, medium within 5 and low within 10.

## Approval matrix
No alert may be closed automatically at Liffey Payments; every closure requires compliance investigator approval. Enhanced monitoring may be applied automatically if verification passed. STR escalations and account restrictions require MLRO approval. Recommendations prepared by a user cannot be approved by the same user.

## Crypto-asset exits
Given Liffey's merchant and consumer base, outbound transfers to crypto-asset service providers exceeding EUR 5,000 in 30 days by consumer customers under 12 months tenure are treated as a high-priority indicator of mule activity.

## AI assistance
AI-generated recommendations are decision support only. Investigators must confirm that every cited transaction and policy reference exists and supports the conclusion.
