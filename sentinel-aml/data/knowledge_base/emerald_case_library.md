# Emerald Digital Bank - Anonymised Case Library 2025

## Case EM-2025-014 - student mule network
A 19-year-old customer with an account open four months received 42 instant payments from 31 different senders over three weeks, forwarding 94% of the funds to a crypto-asset exchange within a day. References included "gift" and "for the car". Two incoming payments were subject to fraud recalls. Outcome: STR filed, outbound payments restricted by the MLRO, relationship exited.

## Case EM-2025-022 - takeaway business lodgements
A takeaway business triggered the structuring rule after two Monday lodgements of EUR 8,900 and EUR 9,400. Deposits were at the same branch every week, the amounts followed weekend trading, supplier and payroll payments were funded from takings, and 12-month cash volume matched declared turnover. Outcome: closed as false positive with documented rationale.

## Case EM-2025-031 - trade references to high-risk jurisdiction
A retail customer with an employee profile received wires from a trading hub and forwarded similar amounts to a high-risk third country within three days, referencing "machinery parts". No trade activity was declared and no invoices were provided on request. Outcome: STR filed and enhanced due diligence initiated.

## Case EM-2025-040 - family support payments
A customer sent monthly payments of about EUR 1,500 to a family member in a high-risk-listed country, referenced as medical costs, consistent with salary income and declared family circumstances. Outcome: closed as false positive; documented and customer kept on standard monitoring.

## Case EM-2025-047 - property completion
A customer received EUR 310,000 from a solicitor client account and paid EUR 295,000 to another solicitor three days later, triggering the rapid movement rule. Outcome: closed as false positive after confirming the property transaction.
