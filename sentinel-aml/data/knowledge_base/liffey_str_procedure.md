# Liffey Payments - Suspicious Transaction Reporting Procedure

## Internal escalation
Staff escalate suspicions to the MLRO through the case management platform. Escalations include the evidence bundle, applicable typology, and a draft narrative that avoids unnecessary personal data.

## Decision and submission
The MLRO decides whether to report within 3 business days. Reports are submitted to FIU Ireland through goAML and to the Revenue Commissioners. Decisions not to report require a written rationale retained for five years.

## Merchant accounts
For merchant customers, the MLRO also considers whether card acquiring should be suspended, in coordination with the scheme compliance team.
