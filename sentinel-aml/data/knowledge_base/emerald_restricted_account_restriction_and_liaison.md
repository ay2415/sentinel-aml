# Emerald Digital Bank - Account Restriction and Law Enforcement Liaison (Restricted)

## Account restriction criteria
The MLRO may restrict outbound payments where there is an ongoing risk of dissipation of suspected criminal proceeds, where victims of fraud are likely to suffer further loss, or where FIU Ireland or An Garda Siochana requests that funds are not released. Restrictions must be proportionate, documented and reviewed within 5 business days.

## Customer communication
Customer-facing staff use only MLRO-approved scripted explanations referring to a routine account review. No reference to suspicion, reporting or investigations may be made, because doing so risks tipping off.

## Law enforcement liaison
Production orders, information requests and freezing directions are received only by the MLRO office. Responses are logged in the restricted case register. Information about these requests must not be stored in general case notes.

## Direction not to proceed
Where FIU Ireland directs the bank not to carry out a transaction or service, the direction is applied immediately and recorded, and the relationship manager is informed only that a hold is in place.
