# Emerald Digital Bank - STR Escalation and Decision Procedure

## Escalation to the MLRO
Investigators escalate when they know, suspect or have reasonable grounds to suspect money laundering or terrorist financing. Escalations must not wait for certainty. The escalation file contains the alert, the evidence summary, the typology assessment, relevant policy references and a draft narrative.

## STR narrative standard
A good narrative answers: who is involved (by case reference), what activity occurred, when and over what period, where funds came from and went to, why the activity is suspicious with reference to specific red flags, and what the bank has done (for example restrictions or enhanced monitoring). Amounts and dates must be accurate and reconcile to system records. Speculation must be labelled as such.

## MLRO decision
The MLRO decides within 5 business days of escalation. If the MLRO decides to report, the report is submitted to FIU Ireland via goAML and to the Revenue Commissioners as soon as practicable. If the MLRO decides not to report, the rationale is recorded and retained for at least five years.

## Post-report handling
After an STR, the customer relationship is reviewed. Continued relationship requires MLRO sign-off and enhanced monitoring. All staff remain subject to the tipping-off prohibition. Law enforcement production orders are handled under the restricted liaison procedure.
